A home server, described entirely in Ansible: file shares, a media server, a photo library, ad-blocking DNS, a VPN back into the house, and this site, served through a Cloudflare tunnel. Irreplaceable files are snapshotted and backed up offsite every night, and every week the backup is restored and checked byte for byte.
